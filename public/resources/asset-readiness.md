## Define the boundary

- [ ] Confirm your organization controls this asset.
- [ ] Record who authorized the proposed security work.
- [ ] Identify third-party services and excluded targets.
- [ ] Define the permitted activity, timing, and limits.
- [ ] Identify sensitive data that must not enter evidence artifacts.

## Register and verify

- [ ] Register the asset in the workspace.
- [ ] Follow the verification instructions shown on its detail page.
- [ ] Confirm the application reports successful verification.
- [ ] Review the controls and availability of the intended workflow.

Proof of control and permission to execute are separate. Registering an asset does not start a scan.
