# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ScopeForge is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or technical specialization.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- be respectful and constructive
- discuss evidence and ideas rather than attacking people
- welcome newcomers and good-faith questions
- give actionable review feedback
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them
- respect security, privacy, and responsible-disclosure boundaries

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private information without permission, deliberate disruption, or encouraging unlawful or unauthorized use of security capabilities.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community spaces, and public interactions when representing ScopeForge.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior violates these expectations. Reports should include enough context for maintainers to assess the situation and will be handled as privately as practical.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted for the ScopeForge security community.
